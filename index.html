About

Evidence-based orthodontics, delivered with care

I'm an MDS Orthodontist and Dentofacial Orthopedician. I trained in Orthodontics at the Indira Gandhi Institute of Dental Sciences, Pondicherry, after 2.5 years in general dental practice.

My work covers orthodontic diagnosis, treatment planning, fixed braces, clear aligners, growth modification for children, and temporary anchorage devices (TADs). I'm an MSA-certified Aligner Specialist, I use both conventional and digital orthodontics, and I publish research on evidence-based orthodontic practice.

  • ✦ Clear explanation and counselling before every treatment
  • ✦ Treatment plans based on the latest clinical evidence
  • ✦ Interdisciplinary care, coordinated with your other dental needs

Languages: English · Tamil · Hindi (basic)

Treatments

How I can help

Braces

Metal and ceramic fixed braces to straighten teeth and correct the bite.

Clear Aligners

Nearly invisible, removable aligners for discreet straightening, planned digitally.

Children's Orthodontics

Early, growth-guiding treatment with functional appliances while the jaws are still developing.

TADs / Mini-Implants

Temporary anchorage devices that make complex tooth movements possible.

Retainers & Retention

Retainers and follow-up visits so your results stay that way for years.

General Dental Care

Check-ups, scaling, fillings, root canals and extractions, from years in general practice.
